01 THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGE

Rooted in Urdu.
Always learning.

I am Iffat Fatima, a teacher and writer with a master’s degree in Urdu. My work grows from a deep love of language, literature, and the many ways a story can bring people closer.

Whether I am guiding students through a poem or shaping a new story, I believe words can open doors: to knowledge, to empathy, and to worlds we have yet to imagine.
